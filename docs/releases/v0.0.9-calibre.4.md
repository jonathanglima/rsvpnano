# RSVP Nano v0.0.9-calibre.4

A fork build of upstream v0.0.9 carrying the Calibre library sync. This release
teaches the sync the difference between a book and an article, and fixes the
build failure that had been blocking automated releases.

## Articles are shelved as articles

The device has always kept books and articles apart on the card — `/library/books`
and `/library/articles` — and drawn them differently: articles get their own spine
palette and a shorter spine on the shelf. Until now only the RSS reader ever wrote
to the articles folder, so anything pulled from Calibre arrived looking like a
novel, web clippings included.

The Calibre sync now routes on a tag:

| Tags in Calibre | Lands in |
|-----------------|----------|
| `rsvp` | `/library/books` |
| `rsvp`, `article` | `/library/articles` |

The two tags are independent: `rsvp` decides whether a book syncs at all, `article`
decides how it is shelved. The tag is matched case-insensitively, since Calibre
keeps whatever case you typed.

Retagging a book you already have is cheap. Adding the tag does not rewrite the
`.rsvp`, so the sync does not re-download it — it renames the file on the card and
carries the reading-progress and index sidecars along, so you keep your place in a
book you were part-way through. Removing the tag moves it back.

## Fixes

- The Calibre engine referenced `SD_MMC` directly, which does not exist on the
  ESP32-C6 board (it mounts its card over SPI). That failed the C6 leg of the
  release build matrix and, with it, the publish step for every board — which is
  why `v0.0.9-calibre.2` and `.3` had their assets attached by hand. Card access
  now goes through `Board::Storage::filesystem()`, the accessor the rest of the
  firmware already uses.
- Deleting a book during a sync removed its `.rsvp` but left the `.rstate.toml`,
  `.ridx` and `.rdat` sidecars behind on the card forever. They are removed with it
  now.

## Upgrading

OTA from the device: Device → check for firmware update. Settings are preserved
within the v0.0.9 line; the settings wipe warned about in `v0.0.9-calibre` applies
only when coming from v0.0.8 or earlier.

Existing books stay where they are until you tag them. The first sync after adding
`article` to a book moves it without downloading anything.
