# RSVP Nano v0.1.2-calibre

A fork build of upstream v0.1.2 carrying the Calibre library sync. It brings in
everything upstream shipped from v0.0.10 through v0.1.2, puts "Sync from Calibre"
on the new watch layouts, and makes the sync leave alone the book you are reading.

## From upstream (v0.0.10 – v0.1.2)

The full notes are in `v0.0.10.md`, `v0.1.0.md`, `v0.1.1.md` and `v0.1.2.md`. In
short:

- Dedicated layouts for watch-sized boards (AMOLED 2.41 V1/V2, 2.16, 2.06, 1.8
  V1/V2, LCD 1.47), drawn in each panel's native orientation.
- An interactive reader appearance editor that replaces the separate reader and
  typography settings pages.
- Em dashes count as word boundaries. Existing books are reindexed once, the first
  time they are opened.
- Companion USB sessions stay open more reliably, and deleting the open book from
  the web app now closes it after a confirmation.
- AMOLED rendering in aligned 32-row bands, plus board wiring, power and touch
  fixes.

## Calibre on watch-sized devices

The new watch Device screen gets a "Sync from Calibre" card next to USB transfer,
Companion sync and Refresh RSS. On the LCD 3.49 the button stays where it was.

## The open book is left alone

The reader keeps the open book's file and its index open. Until now a sync could
still delete it (removed from Calibre, Mirror policy), move it (retagged as an
article) or overwrite it (edited in Calibre) while you were reading it.

The sync now skips any action on the open book, just as the companion app refuses
to delete an open book without confirmation. The book stays where it is and its
entry in the sync manifest is unchanged. The first sync after you close it carries
the action out. When this happens, the completion status ends with ", 1 in use".

## Fixes

- A finished Calibre sync showed its result under an "OTA" title and did not
  refresh the library screen. The library only rebuilds its listing when the
  number of books changes, so an article retag (same count, new folder) left a
  stale entry that failed to open. The status is now titled "Calibre" and the
  library refreshes after every sync.

## Upgrading

OTA from the device: Device → check for firmware update.

Coming from v0.0.9-calibre.N, all settings are kept except the reader visibility
choices (battery icon and label, chapter, progress). Upstream replaced those on/off
switches with `always`, `reading`, `paused` and `never` in v0.1.0. They go back to
their defaults, so check them in the appearance editor after updating. Calibre
settings and the sync manifest are untouched, so the first sync downloads nothing
new.
